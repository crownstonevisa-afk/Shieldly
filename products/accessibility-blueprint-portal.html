Notes on building work

The portal supplies plans, assessments and coordination — it does not itself perform construction. Building work should always be carried out by licensed contractors in your area, and structural, electrical or plumbing changes must comply with local building codes and permits. Laam's plans are a starting point for professional review, not a substitute for a licensed architect or engineer where your jurisdiction requires one.

Questions about this suite

Do I need a contractor to use the blueprints?

For simple adaptations, the library alone may be enough to guide a handyman. For structural or systems work, you will want a licensed contractor — and the Coordination Suite is built for exactly that handoff.

Can plans be adapted to my actual home?

Yes. Blueprints are starting points with adaptable dimensions, and the Plan Studio builds plans from your own assessment findings and room measurements.

Does the price include construction costs?

No. Prices are for the software portal and its content only. Construction, materials and contractor fees are separate and agreed with your chosen contractor.
