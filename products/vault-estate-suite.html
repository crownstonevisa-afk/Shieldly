Security notes

Vault contents are encrypted in transit and at rest, held in a private dedicated portal, and never sold or shared with advertisers. Questions about this suite

Can I start with Core and upgrade later?

Yes — editions build on one another, and the records you organise in Core carry forward. Email us and we will confirm the upgrade arrangement for your situation.

Who owns the records in the vault?

You do. Laam never claims ownership of your documents, photographs or family history, and never sells or shares them.

What happens to the vault after the owner passes away?

That is exactly what the permissions and release rules are for: you decide in advance who gains access, to what, and when, so executors and heirs are not left searching.
