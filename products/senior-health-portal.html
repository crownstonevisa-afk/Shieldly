Security & care notes

Health information is among the most private data there is. Portals are private and dedicated, encrypted in transit and at rest, and access is limited to the people the family or organisation authorises. We never sell health data or share it with advertisers. This software supports caregiving and monitoring; it is not a medical device, it does not diagnose or treat, and it is not a substitute for professional medical advice or emergency services — in an emergency, always call local emergency services first.

Questions about this suite

Does this replace a doctor or emergency service?

No. The portal supports monitoring and coordination. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace professional care, and it must never be relied on in an emergency.

Can several family members use one portal?

Yes. Caregiver Access is built for families, with individual logins and permissions for each person involved.

Is my loved one's health data sold or shared?

Never. Health data stays inside the private portal, visible only to authorised people, and is never sold to advertisers or third parties.
