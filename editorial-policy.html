Editorial Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

How Articles Are Written

Every Shieldly article is planned around a real owner question, researched from primary sources — standard policy wordings, state insurance department and regulator guidance, and insurer documentation — and written in plain English by or under the direction of our founder, Muhammad Sameer, Cyber Security Consultant. Cyber content reflects professional security practice. We write original content; we do not copy, scrape, or spin material from other websites.

Accuracy and Estimates

We state what policies typically cover and exclude, and we label every cost figure as a general educational estimate with its basis. Where rules vary by state or by insurer, we say so rather than pretending a single national answer exists.

Review and Updates

Articles carry a publication or update date and are reviewed periodically and whenever rules, standard forms, or market practice materially change. Updates revise the article in place with a new date.

Corrections

If we get something wrong, we correct it openly in the article. Readers can report errors via the Contact page; confirmed corrections are made promptly.

Independence

Shieldly sells no insurance and accepts no payment from insurers to write or rank content. Advertising on the Site, including Google AdSense, is separated from editorial decisions. Any future sponsored or affiliate content will be clearly labelled and will not alter our conclusions.

Use of Technology

We may use software tools for drafting support, editing, and production, but every published article is human-reviewed for accuracy, originality, and usefulness before it appears on the Site, and a named author stands behind it.
