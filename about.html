About Shieldly

Shieldly was founded by Muhammad Sameer, a Cyber Security Consultant, with a simple frustration: small business owners are expected to buy insurance — some of it legally required, most of it contractually demanded — through documents written in language almost designed to be misunderstood. Quotes arrive without explanations, exclusions hide in schedules, and cyber cover is sold with either fear or jargon. Shieldly exists to fix the explanation part.

Our Story

Working in cyber security means seeing the worst week of a business's life up close: the locked systems, the diverted payment, the customer data in the wrong hands. Again and again, the financial outcome of those weeks turned on decisions made years earlier in an afternoon — a policy bought, a limit chosen, a control skipped. Shieldly brings that incident-room experience to insurance education, so owners can make those decisions calmly, in advance, with full information.

What We Do

We publish free, in-depth, plain-English guides to US small business insurance and cyber protection: what each policy covers and excludes, realistic educational cost ranges, buying checklists, claims walkthroughs, and a working cyber security checklist drawn from professional practice. We do not sell insurance, and we are not a broker, agent, or comparison site. Our reader is the owner who wants to understand first and buy second.

How Our Content Is Researched and Reviewed

Every guide starts from primary material — standard policy forms, state insurance department guidance, and regulator and insurer documentation — and is checked against real claims and incident patterns. Cost figures are presented as general educational estimates with their basis stated, never as quotes. Articles are reviewed before publication for accuracy and plain language, dated, and revisited when rules, market practice, or our guidance changes. Corrections are made openly and noted; see our Editorial Policy for the full standard.

About the Founder

Muhammad Sameer — Cyber Security Consultant, founder of Shieldly. Based in Lahore, Pakistan, and serving US readers online, Muhammad works in cyber security consulting for small businesses: the audits, controls, and incident preparation that decide whether an attack is an inconvenience or a catastrophe. Shieldly is where that expertise meets insurance literacy, because in a real incident the controls and the cover have to work together.

Our Promise

  • Plain English first; jargon defined the moment it appears.
  • Exclusions and limitations stated as clearly as benefits.
  • Cost ranges labelled as educational estimates, always.
  • No invented statistics, no fake reviews, no pressure to buy anything from us — we sell nothing.
