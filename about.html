About Laam

A quiet company for life's most important records.

Laam Digital Vault & Estate Archiving Suite is a UK-based technology company. We build private software suites for clients in the United States — for families organising an estate, for caregivers looking after a senior loved one, and for homeowners making a house safe for the years ahead.

Built for the moments that matter.

Estate, health and home — the three parts of life that deserve a serious record.

Our mission

Most of what a family owns that truly matters is not furniture or money. It is the record: the deed to the house, the will, the photograph of a grandparent, the note about a parent's medication, the plan that made a bathroom safe. Those records are almost always scattered, and they are almost always needed most urgently at the worst possible moment — during illness, after a bereavement, in the middle of a move.

Our mission is simple: give every family a single, private, trustworthy place for those records, built with the same seriousness a bank gives a vault. Not a social feed, not an advertising platform, not a subscription that rents your own history back to you month after month. A portal that is yours, bought once, and built to last.

What we believe

Privacy first

Your family's records are not a product. We do not sell data, we do not share it with advertisers, and we do not use it to train models. Portals are private and dedicated, and access is governed by permissions you set.

Ownership, not rental

Every suite is a one-time purchase. The licence, the portal and the records inside it belong to the client — not to a monthly billing cycle.

Permanence

Archives, health timelines and home records are meant to last for years, even generations. We build for durability: careful storage, exportable records, and formats that do not trap you.

Clarity over cleverness

Our software is designed for people under stress, for older users, and for families who are not technical. If a feature cannot be explained simply, it does not belong in the product.

Human purchase, human support

Because these are significant purchases, every sale is arranged personally by email. Questions are answered by people, before and after purchase, and enquiries are always free.

Built for the United States

Although Laam is based in the United Kingdom, our suites are designed for clients in the United States — their homes, their care arrangements, their estate practices and their everyday realities.

How the suites are delivered

Each suite is delivered as a private, dedicated software portal, provisioned for the purchasing family or organisation and configured to their needs. Access is granted to exactly the people the client chooses — family members, executors, trustees, caregivers, clinicians or contractors, depending on the suite — and every portal carries an audit trail of who accessed what and when.

Delivery begins with an email enquiry to admin@laam.buzz. We confirm the right product and edition, agree the one-time price and the arrangement, and then provision the portal. There is no checkout on this website and no card payment is processed here; purchases of this size are handled personally, by email, so that nothing is misunderstood and nothing is rushed.

What makes Laam different

  • Three suites, one philosophy: estate, health and home are treated as parts of one life, with the same standard of privacy and care.
  • Private by architecture: dedicated portals, not shared public accounts.
  • No advertising, no data resale: our revenue comes from the software itself, not from our clients' information.
  • One-time pricing: clear, published, one-time prices — no subscriptions for the licence itself.
  • Designed for hard days: calm, readable, usable software for illness, loss and change.
