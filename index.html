Chapter 01 · The problem

Life's most important records are scattered everywhere.

Wills, deeds, insurance policies, medical notes, photographs, passwords, property plans — the record of a family's life is spread across drawers, old laptops, email inboxes and filing cabinets. When something happens — an illness, a move, a bereavement — the people who need those records most cannot find them, and the stress of searching falls on exactly the people least able to bear it.

Laam exists to end that scramble. Each of our suites gathers one part of a family's life into a single, private, beautifully organised portal, protected by serious security and owned outright by the people it serves.

Chapter 05 · Security & privacy

A vault in name and in engineering.

These suites hold the most sensitive material a family owns, so security is not a feature — it is the foundation. Every suite is delivered as a private, dedicated portal, not a shared public account. Access is controlled by permissions you set, activity is logged, and data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

  • Private, dedicated portals — your suite is yours alone, not pooled with other clients.
  • Encryption in transit and at rest for stored documents, health information and plans.
  • Granular permissions — decide exactly who sees what, down to individual folders and records.
  • No data resale, ever — your information is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used to train models.
  • Full data ownership — your records remain yours, and can be exported on request.
  • Audit trails — see who accessed what, and when, across the portal.
