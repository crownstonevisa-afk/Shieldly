# Laam Digital Vault & Estate Archiving Suite

Live site: https://laam.buzz/

A UK-based technology company serving clients in the United States. The site
showcases three private software suites (15 products in total, each priced
$10,000–$50,000 one-time):

- Private Digital Vault & Family Estate Archiving Suite
- Custom AI Senior Health & Tele-Care Monitoring Portal
- Senior-Accessibility Architectural Blueprint & Remodeling Portal

There is no on-site checkout: every purchase enquiry goes by email to
admin@laam.buzz, and payment is arranged privately by email.

## Structure

- `index.html` — flagship scroll-telling page (vertical story + horizontal
  product journey)
- `products.html` — all three suites with their 5 products each
- `products/` — one page per suite
- `about.html`, `contact.html`, `privacy-policy.html`, `terms.html`,
  `disclaimer.html`
- `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt`, `ads.txt`, `vercel.json`
