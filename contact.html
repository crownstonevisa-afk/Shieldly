Send an enquiry

Fill in this form and press Send Enquiry. It does not submit anything to a server — it opens your own email app with the enquiry already addressed to admin@laam.buzz, ready for you to press Send. That way your message goes directly from your mailbox to ours.

Before you write

How quickly will I hear back?

We aim to reply within 2 business days. If your matter is urgent, say so in the subject line and we will prioritise it.

Can I buy by email?

Yes — that is the only way to buy. Tell us the product and edition, and we will confirm the one-time price and arrange the purchase privately. We do not process card payments on this website for purchases of this size.

Is there a phone number or live chat?

No. Email is our only contact channel, by design: it keeps a clear written record of every arrangement, which matters when the purchase is significant and the records are sensitive.

Do you charge for advice before purchase?

No. Enquiries and product advice before purchase are free, and we will tell you honestly if a smaller edition is enough for your needs.

What should I include in a purchase enquiry?

Your name, your organisation if any, the product and edition you want, and anything about your situation that affects the choice. The more you tell us, the better our recommendation.
