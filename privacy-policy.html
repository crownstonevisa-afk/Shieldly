Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Laam ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit shieldly.vercel.app (the "Site"). By using the Site you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

Information you provide: when you use our contact or newsletter forms, we receive the name, email address, and message you submit. Our forms currently display a confirmation on the Site; form submissions should also be sent to us by email if you need a guaranteed record.

Information collected automatically: like most websites, our hosting and analytics providers may log IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, referring URLs, and timestamps. Cookies and similar technologies are used as described below.

Cookies

We and our partners use cookies to operate the Site, remember preferences, understand readership, and serve advertising. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling cookies may affect some features.

Google AdSense and Google Advertising Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display advertising. Google and its third-party vendors use cookies — including the DART cookie and the Google advertising cookies described in Google's policies — to serve ads based on your prior visits to this Site and other sites on the Internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this Site and/or other sites.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings (ads.google.com/settings). You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info. For more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see Google's Privacy & Terms pages.

Third-Party Vendors and Services

We may use hosting, analytics, and advertising providers who process data on our behalf under their own privacy policies. The Site may link to external websites we do not control; their privacy practices are their own.

How We Use Information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Site and its content.
  • To respond to messages and send the newsletter you requested.
  • To measure readership and understand which guides help owners most.
  • To display advertising, including personalized advertising where permitted.
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect the Site's security.

GDPR — Visitors in the European Economic Area

Where the GDPR applies, our legal bases include consent (for non-essential cookies and personalized ads), legitimate interests (site operation and measurement), and contract or legal obligation where relevant. You have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection. Where consent is required for advertising cookies, personalized advertising will only be served in accordance with applicable consent requirements. Contact us to exercise any right.

CCPA / CPRA — California Residents

California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion, to correct inaccurate information, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information as those terms are defined by California law. Advertising cookies may constitute sharing for cross-context behavioural advertising; you may opt out via the Google Ads Settings and aboutads.info links above, by using browser-based opt-out signals where honoured, or by contacting us. We do not discriminate against anyone exercising these rights.

Data Retention

We retain contact messages for as long as needed to respond and keep reasonable records, newsletter details until you unsubscribe, and server and analytics logs for a limited period consistent with provider defaults, after which they are deleted or anonymised.

Children

The Site is written for business owners and is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes and Contact

We may update this policy and will post the new version here with a revised date. Questions or rights requests: please use the contact details on our Contact page. Laam, Lahore, Pakistan — serving US readers online.
