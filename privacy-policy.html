Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. This policy explains what information this website collects, and what it does not.

1. Who we are

Laam Digital Vault & Estate Archiving Suite is a UK-based technology company providing private software suites to clients in the United States. Our only contact channel is email: admin@laam.buzz.

2. Information we collect

This website itself collects almost nothing. There is no account system, no newsletter signup and no analytics that identifies individual visitors.

  • Email enquiries: when you write to admin@laam.buzz — directly, or via a "Buy Now" or contact button that opens your email app — we receive the name, email address and message content you choose to send. We use it only to answer you and, if you proceed, to arrange and deliver your purchase.
  • Purchase correspondence: if you buy a suite, we keep the correspondence needed to provision your portal, confirm the arrangement and support you afterwards.
  • Portal data: data you later place inside your own private portal (documents, health information, plans) is held inside that dedicated portal under the access rules you set. It is not used for any other purpose.

3. Information we do not collect on this website

  • No payment card details, bank details or payment credentials are collected on this website. Payment for purchases is arranged privately by email; we do not process card payments on this website for purchases of this size.
  • We do not run advertising on this website and do not share visitor data with advertisers.
  • We do not sell personal data, and we do not use your data to train models.

4. Cookies

This website uses no tracking cookies and no advertising cookies. Any strictly necessary technical storage used by the hosting platform to serve pages securely is not used to profile visitors.

5. How your information is protected

Enquiry emails are handled on standard, access-controlled business email systems. Portal data is encrypted in transit and at rest, held in a private dedicated portal, and governed by permissions you control. No system is perfectly secure, and email itself is not an encrypted channel — please do not send highly sensitive documents by ordinary email unless we have first agreed a secure transfer method with you.

6. Sharing

We share personal information only where necessary to operate: with the hosting and email providers that deliver this website and your correspondence, under their own security obligations, and where required by law. We never sell personal data.

7. Your rights and choices

You may ask us at any time to confirm what enquiry correspondence we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, by writing to admin@laam.buzz. We will respond within 2 business days. Data inside a purchased portal can be exported on request, and deletion of a portal is handled by agreement at the end of the licence arrangement.

8. Children

This website and its products are intended for adults and organisations. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date. Continued use of the website after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

For any privacy question, write to admin@laam.buzz.
